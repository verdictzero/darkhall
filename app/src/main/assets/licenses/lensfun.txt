Lens profile: lensfun database

The distortion and lateral chromatic aberration coefficients Darkhall uses for the lens of the
Olympus Tough TG-5, TG-6 and TG-7 come from the lensfun database ("Olympus Tough TG-5" in
compact-olympus.xml), measured on a TG-5:
  https://lensfun.github.io

Darkhall uses the coefficients unchanged.
